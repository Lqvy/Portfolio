To put a face to the name, here I am. As far as introductions go; Hi! My name is Chase, I was born early 2003, and the work shown within this portfolio is code with visualization analyzing various datasets. I have data analyses here. As well as an about me for a short history.

About

How my interest started.

About me... I've had an interest in technology since I was young, I played lots of video games but never truly got into physical technology until my later years. Upon my first part-time job, I built my own computer, this kickstarted my true interest in the topic. From here, I later got interested in game indie dev'ing and 3D art.

Continued..

From my early interest in indie dev came my interest in code for it's structure and abstract thought. As I got older I wanted something I could do more professionally but still retain consistently learning with creative application. So, out of the sea of things to choose from, Data Analytics arose. Data Analytics allows me to do deep dives and statistically back any informative findings, which I can then visualize as a coherent story for dashboards, and the likes.

Education.

I was in traditional high school for about 2 years, then I was introduced to Polk State Lakeland Gateway to College Collegiate High School, in which I started college full-time. I had my AA degree a semester after highschool, and my bachelors 3 years after that. I am continuing my education with UCF doing the Masters Data Analytics program.

New Yet Defining Interests.

Through my time pursuing these ventures I've gained other interests in backpacking, traveling, philosophy, and psychology. Philosophy has massively changed the way I approach coding, as philosophy is great for learning in depth critical thinking, similar to code. As well as psychology changing how I approach ML and AI, as psychology is the study of the mind and behavior, which is what ML/AI attempts to mimic.
